# Glossary — 瘋狂大富翁 (Crazy Tycoon) English

Tone: light, jokey, kid-friendly Taiwanese board-game humor (Momotaro Dentetsu-style). Keep the puns and parodies alive;
prefer short punchy lines over literal ones. US English.
