# Crazy Tycoon English patch v1.0.1

> **AI translation.** This patch was made by an AI model (Anthropic's Claude), directed and play-tested by a human,
> and not reviewed by a fluent human translator. Please support human fan translators and their projects.

## Fixed

- Text that followed a scrolling speech in the same box (narration after the Wealth God speaks, results after a
  typhoon or other disaster report) could print its lines bottom-first and then wrap to the top. It now always
  starts on the top line.

## Patching

Patch in the browser at https://gopherbone.github.io/fengkuangdafuweng_en/, or apply `crazy-tycoon-en.ips` to:

- `Feng Kuang Da Fu Weng (Unlicensed, Chinese) (Multicart Rip) [Header Fix].gbc`
- 2,097,152 bytes, CRC32 `AD68B3DD`, SHA-1 `72d3b05ca1bd08b646c3a3240fcc29f630958825`

The patched ROM should have CRC32 `0590E59E`.
